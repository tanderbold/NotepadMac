# speaker has to decide: file, English text, kind, term, tab-separated.
#   term       the text has none of the words the official nativeLang file uses for the term
#   identical  the text is the English one although the official file translates its words
#   english    an English word left inside the translated text
# Many are a word in another sense or form than the official file's (the adjective "open" for
# the verb, the spelling's "Language" for the programming one) and fine as they are; some are
# another word for the same thing. Deciding one: change the text to the official word, or leave
# the Item out (it stays English), or add a fifth column "right". Then
# python3 macos/check_nativelang_terms.py --pending rewrites this list (header and "right" kept).
# A finding not listed here fails macos/test.sh.
